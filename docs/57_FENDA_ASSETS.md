# Arte da auditoria da Fenda Pelágica

Gerada com a ferramenta nativa `image_gen`, sem API/CLI de geração. Os arquivos finais estão em [assets/arcade](../public/assets/arcade/), e a procedência/hash está no [manifesto](../public/assets/arcade/pelagic-manifest.json). O [empacotador](../scripts/pack-pelagic-art.py) apenas recorta células, redimensiona e registra o atlas; preserva o alfa produzido pela ferramenta. Nenhuma pintura ou remoção de fundo foi feita por script.

## Atlas costeiro — prompt de criação

> Create a production game sprite atlas, stylized-concept illustration. 4 columns by 2 rows, exactly EIGHT distinct independent assets in equal square cells, 2048x1024 overall, each asset fully contained with transparent padding. True transparent background. Modern vibrant hand drawn cartoon/anime tropical vertical shooter, crisp ink outlines, beautiful detailed cel shading, sunlight from upper left, orthographic overhead view, no horizon, no text, no frames. Row1: 1 oval tropical sandy island with palms and grey rocks (no buildings), 2 DIFFERENT asymmetric tropical island with rock ledges and palms, 3 oval rocky island with a red white striped lighthouse upright in image, 4 small reef cluster underwater turquoise rocks with subtle translucent cyan shallows. Row2: 1 LEFT SHORE harbor platform with blue roof warehouse, yellow crane and dock fingers extending toward RIGHT; 2 RIGHT SHORE harbor platform with blue roof warehouse and yellow crane and dock fingers extending toward LEFT, independently drawn not a mirrored copy; 3 standalone small rocky tropical sandbank with two palm trees; 4 standalone small yellow crane on concrete dock platform. Dock/island shapes predominantly oval and compact, no wide projecting thin unrelated pieces. Coastal foam hugs the land in a fine white rim and localized cyan transparent shallows, but NO ocean rectangle, NO background water, NO boats or vehicles, NO shadows outside cells. Consistent correctly upright orientation of every lighthouse/crane/building, no inverted or rotated structures. Rich clean readable detail for use at 90-140 pixels each in game. This is an atlas of SEPARATE map objects, never a full scene. Each object occupies central 75% of its 512x512 cell.

> Precise background-extraction edit of this EIGHT sprite game atlas. Preserve all eight islands, docks, reef, lighthouse, palms, cranes and their exact 4-column 2-row positions and sizes. Remove ALL broad grey/white/pale translucent haze around each island and object. REMOVE the square/rectangular water patches and grey washed-out halos reaching the cell edges. Each island must have ONLY a very narrow white foam shore outline and a narrow 5-10px cyan shore rim hugging its silhouette, outside that absolutely zero alpha completely transparent empty padding. No broad glow. The reef should keep its colored rocks and have a narrow turquoise silhouette edge only. All pixels between objects and on the outer atlas border must be fully transparent. Preserve all object detail and bright cartoon colors. Do not draw any checkerboard or colored backdrop. Isolated clean game sprites. True transparent alpha output.

## Água — prompt final

Saída: [pelagic-water.webp](../public/assets/arcade/pelagic-water.webp). Redimensionamento mecânico para 1024 × 1024, WebP lossless.

> A production seamless tileable ocean surface texture for a vibrant modern 2D cartoon anime vertical arcade shooter. Square 1024x1024. Strict overhead view, no horizon, no land, no islands, no rocks, no boats, no structures, no text. Rich deep royal blue water with hand-drawn short irregular curved wave facets, gentle medium blue and dark cobalt interlocking shapes, small sparse light azure crest edges and a very few white foam glints. Beautiful crisp cel shaded painted water, lively but calm enough to read yellow player bullets and red enemy bullets. No giant patches, no fog, no huge foam, no spirals, no geometric vector chevrons, no identical repeated oval scribbles. All four edges must tile seamlessly: continuous matching wave shapes and identical illumination across left/right and top/bottom edges. Uniform average deep blue color across entire tile. Not a whole scene: only the open ocean WATER material, suitable for repeating scrolling TileSprite.
